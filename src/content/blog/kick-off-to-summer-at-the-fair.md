We all know that the Minnesota State Fair got canceled last year and the cravings for all those fair foods are going wild!  No need to worry, you don’t have to wait until August to enjoy all that good food.  The [Kick Off to Summer](https://www.mnstatefair.org/kickoff-to-summer) event at the fair is helping to satisfy those cravings for some folks and gives us a preview of what’s to come.
